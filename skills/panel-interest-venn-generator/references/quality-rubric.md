# Quality Rubric (1-100)

Score each category, then add them up. A run scoring under 70 should be improved
before it's shared. **Overlap analysis carries 40 points**, so a beautiful diagram with a
shallow analysis cannot score above 60.

| # | Category | Points |
|---|----------|-------:|
| 1 | Overlap analysis | 40 |
| 2 | Research accuracy and sourcing | 15 |
| 3 | MicroSim correctness and usability | 20 |
| 4 | Documentation (`index.md`) | 10 |
| 5 | Intro email | 10 |
| 6 | Knowledge graph and feedback loop | 5 |
| | **Total** | **100** |

## 1. Overlap analysis (40)
- 8: Interests are normalized into concepts, with a visible mapping table.
- 8: The membership matrix has evidence (a source) for every cell marked true.
- 8: Every overlap region shown has a one-sentence explanation of *why* those people meet there.
- 8: Overlaps are ranked, and the 3-5 strongest become discussion themes tied to the panel topic.
- 4: Near-overlaps and bridge opportunities are identified.
- 4: There is at least one all-shared concept, or an explicit statement that none exists, with reasoning.

## 2. Research accuracy and sourcing (15)
- 5: Every panelist has 5 or more interests with source URLs.
- 5: There are no unsupported claims; uncertain identities are flagged with a confidence level.
- 5: Emails are verified or marked missing, never guessed.

## 3. MicroSim (20)
- 5: It renders with no console errors, and the circles and names are legible.
- 5: No labels overlap (verified with a screenshot).
- 4: The hover tooltip names the sharers, and gold marks all-shared concepts.
- 3: The canvas is width-responsive.
- 3: The Send Feedback link works.

## 4. Documentation (10)
- 4: Sources are working Markdown links (not bare text).
- 3: The page has Description, How to Use, and a Lesson Plan.
- 3: There's a preview image and a nav entry.

## 5. Intro email (10)
- 3: Full names are in To, and every panelist is greeted.
- 4: It names the top overlap themes and links the live MicroSim.
- 3: It explains how to update interests; it's saved as a draft and not sent.

## 6. Knowledge graph and feedback loop (5)
- 3: Person and Event nodes are added or handed off.
- 2: The feedback inbox or routine is wired up.

## Bands
- 90-100: Insightful, ready to share.
- 70-89: Good; fix the gaps listed.
- 50-69: The diagram works but the analysis is thin.
- Below 50: Redo the research and analysis.
