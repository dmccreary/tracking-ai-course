## Description
This MicroSim shows the research interests of the four panelists at the Twin Cities ACM *AI Panel Discussion and Networking* (Oct 28, 2026, MSU Mankato–Edina). Each person is a translucent circle. Interests sit in the region of everyone who shares them, and gold labels mark the interests all four share (agentic AI and teaching AI). Hover over any label to see who shares it.

## How to Use
1. Move the mouse over an interest label.
2. A tooltip lists the panelists who share it.
3. Resize the window. The canvas is width-responsive.
