# Eight AI Forces — Image Generation Prompt

Please generate a new image.

## Important Note on Text

Unlike most diagram-overlay MicroSims, **this image is intended to include short text labels and icons baked into the artwork**. The interactive overlay sits on top of the labeled icons as clickable hotspots — the background image must clearly identify each of the eight forces by name and icon so the user knows what they are clicking. Keep all text crisp, sans-serif, and minimal. Do not add explanatory paragraphs, arrows with words, or decorative typography.

---

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: PNG
- **Dimensions**: 1400 × 900 px (landscape, ~14:9 ratio)
- **Background**: Very light off-white / cream (#FBFAF7) with a faint hexagonal or circuit-pattern texture, low contrast (no more than 6% darker than the background — should feel like a watermark)
- **Style**: Clean, modern infographic illustration, flat vector style with subtle gradients. Suitable for a business/strategy textbook. Think Stripe Press / FT Visual Storytelling — restrained palette, generous whitespace, professional but friendly.

## What to Draw

A symmetrical infographic poster titled **"Eight AI Forces"** at the top, with eight labeled force icons arranged in two vertical columns (4 left, 4 right) flanking a central circular medallion containing a friendly robot. The four left-side forces are color-coded warm (orange/red family — they push capability up). The four right-side forces are color-coded cool (blue/teal/green family — they hold capability back). The composition should read at a glance: "four forces pushing in, central AI, four forces pushing back."

## Layout Notes

- The composition is strongly symmetric. Left and right columns must mirror each other vertically (same y positions for rows 1–4 on both sides).
- Maintain at least 60px of clear space around every text label — the overlay markers (small colored dots, ~24px) will sit on top of each icon badge, and surrounding text must not be cluttered.
- The central medallion is the visual anchor — it should be ~30% larger than any of the eight icon badges.
- Background texture (the faint hex/circuit pattern) should never overpower the icons or text. If in doubt, make it lighter.
- Do **not** add any other decorative elements: no random gears, no brain illustration, no neural-network sketches, no city skylines, no hand-drawn flourishes. The eight icons + central robot + title are the entire scene.
- Do **not** add any caption, footer, source line, or watermark.
- Allowed text: only the title, subtitle, "AI Model Capability" label, and the eight two-line force labels described above. No other text anywhere in the image.
