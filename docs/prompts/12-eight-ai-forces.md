# Eight AI Forces


## Prompt
Use the /interactive-infographic-overlay skill to create a new microsim   
  called 'eight-ai-forces'.  In this microsim you will have four forces on the  
  left and four forces on the right.  In the center you will have a circle  
  with a picture of a robot in in.  Above the circle is the title "AI Model     
  Capability".  The names of the forces can be drawn from                       
  @docs/articles/winner-takes-all.md The title at the top of the MicroSim is
  "Eight AI Forces".  Use the dual panel layout.  When the user clicks on a     
  force icon it will have an infobox show details about he force.  The          
  background image will be created by a text-to-image LLM that you will         
  describe in detail.  Keep the image clean, but have a clear label and icon    
  for each of the eight forces.
